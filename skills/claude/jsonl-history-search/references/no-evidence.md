# Weak or no evidence

Run direct `jsonl-history-search ask "<original question>" --cwd "$PWD" --format text`.
Inspect `scope`, `coverage` and `omissions`, not packet readiness.

`not_found` means no lexical match in fully read selected sources, not absence
from all history. `empty_scope`, `unavailable`, malformed sources and capped
reads are different outcomes. Natural date/local-time wording may be unresolved;
fully read selected sources do not establish that every requested qualifier was
applied. A no-overlap paraphrase can be missed by the local lexical selector.

Answer supported facts and name the remaining gap. Use targeted native reads
when concrete relevant evidence remains available within authorized resources;
do not repeat sufficient evidence or restart broad loops. Do not expand source
permission merely because the first result is weak.
